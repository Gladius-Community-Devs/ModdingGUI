So going left to right, some of this i think is already known.
"" - character autofills that slot. if you don't have that character, it's just locked out (you could fill it with anything, like require barbeeleen in every battle)
100 - i believe the %chance for an enemy to appear (or in the player case, whether or not that is a required spot)
Start1 - what "starting area" you get to choose for that character.
1 - character must be higher than or equal to this level
2 - character must be lower than or equal to this level
0 - this is 0 or 1; sets whether or not character must be equal to your school level (so if your school level is 5, it'll make the rquirement "level 5 to 5")
4 - attribute requirement, so 4 would be "heavy" required.
5 - sets affnity requirement, so 5 would be fire
"Archer, "Amazon", "Bandit", "Yeti" - sets class requirements. So this would require you to pick an archer, amazon, bandit, or yeti.

6-14 are usually just set to 0 or 1 (slot 12 being an exception, 6, 10, 13, and 14 might also be exceptions but i'm unsure of what they do), i just gave them those numbers to make them easier to point out.

6 - unsure
7 - flips the classess to prohibited instead of required (so archer, amazon, bandit, yeti would be prohibited in the example)
8 - flips the attribute to prohibited instead of required, so heavy would be prohibited instead of required in the example
9 - flips the affinity to prohibited
10 - unsure
11 - requires a mercenary (temp recruit)
12 - chance per item to drop chest
13 - unsure
14 - unsure




`CUSTOMIZE:` line in a school file information: Max outfit = number of <mesh>_varNN.ptx files in data/characters/<mesh>/. Every skeleton mesh (skeletonSpl1/2, skeletonNor1/2, skeletonImp1/2, skeletonSte1/2, skeletonExp1/2) has no var files, so any undead unit must use CUSTOMIZE: 0, "".
