Skill Effects

SKILLEFFECT: "effect", 100, 1

This is required before you can do ANY status. "effect" is one of the options below, 100 is chance for it to apply, 1 is dependent on the effect.

There is also SKILLSPLITEFFECT: if you want to have a skill do a different effect based on a condition

Status Only - used for most status effects when you only want the status. The 1 generally doesn't do much since the status itself takes care of most things.
Change Stack Relative - moves a gladiator up or down in the stack relative to their current position. This CAN break the game if you try to move people forward in the stack past where their turn would have been. The 1 is how far up or down in the stack they move (i believe by a %, so .3 would be 30% change)
Change Stack Absolute - honestly couldn't find a difference between this and above, but stack is weird anyway so there could be a difference.
Change HP Relative - Not sure what the difference between relative and absolute is, but this does a % change in HP (used in most healing skills). 1 is how much to heal. Can also do negative numbers to remove HP
Change SP Relative - Not sure what the difference between relative and absolute is, but this does a flat change to SP. 1 is how much to change (SP is by 10's, so putting "10" here would change SP by 1). Can also do negative numbers to remove SP
Change HP Absolute - See above, i couldn't find any difference.
Change SP Absolute - See above, i couldn't find any difference
Divvy Up HP - Kills the character and divvy's up their remaining HP to anybody in the area (Galdr's cry of sacrifice used this before i changed it). The 1 does nothing.
Vampiric HP - leech life, the 1 is a % of how much life you heal from damage (so .1 would be 10% life leech)
Counterattack - Does a counterattack based on whatever the subskill attached is. 1 is how many times you do the counter attack
Group Attack - Group attack (pack mentality). 1 does nothing as far as i can tell
Group Sequential Attack - Group attack that works weird. Initially i thought it chained until somebody failed the roll, but I've had it go for eternity before and completely break the game, so don't use this.
Shield Block - just used in the "block" level 0 skills for people with shields, you can ignore it.
Knockback - knocks an enemy back, the 1 is how many tiles they are knocked back
Knockdown - knocks an enemy down, i don't believe the 1 has any effect.
Preswitch - no idea. I tried to play with it but couldn't figure it out.
Random Teleport - basically just Tornado. The 1 is how many tiles they be moved.
Shield Restore - Ursula's shield restore, i don't think the 1 does anything.
Shield Break - Breaks a shield, the 1 doesn't do anything
Helmet Break - Breaks a helmet, the 1 doesn't do anything
change off affinity - flat change to an offensive affinity (up or down), the 1 is how much to move (each bubble is 20, so putting 30 would be a bubble and a half)
change def affinity - flat change to a defensive affinity out of 100, up or down, the 1 is how much to move.
change either affinity - flat change to either affinity (i think it looks at offensive first then overflows to defensive?). Same as above otherwise.
drain off affinity - flat drain offensive affinity (like channeler drain affinity, but only looks at offensive). Number is how much to steal.
drain def affinity - flat drain defensive affinity (like channeler drain affinity, but only looks at defensive). Number is how much to steal.
drain either affinity - flat drain either affinity. Number is how much to steal.
Change Crowd Source - Change the crowd meter for you. Number is how much to change out of 100.
Change Crowd Target - Change the crowd meter for your target (could be enemy or ally), out of 100.
ShiftReturn - cancels forms.
ShiftTo - go into form
Remove Status - remove a status
Summon - summon a creature
retreat - makes target move away. 1 is number to move.

Skill Statuses

SKILLSTATUS: "status", 100, 1

Status is from option below, 100 is chance to apply, 1 is dependent on the effect. You can also do SKILLSTATUS2: "status", 100, 1 to add a second status to a skill

hpregen noncumulative - hp regen, 1 is how much per tick (not sure what noncumulative means)
damage multiply - didn't play with this one, i assume just a flat damage multiplier buff/debuff though (either damage done or damage taken)
set step down limit - how far down you can jump (not really used), 1 is how much
set step up limit - how far up you can jump, 1 is how much (used on cats). Only really works on innates.
change off affinity charge rate - flat change to how much affinity you gain offensively.
mult off affinity charge rate - percent change to how much affinity you gain offensively.
change def affinity charge rate - flat change to how much affinity you gain defensively
mult def affinity charge rate - percent change to how much affinity you gain defensively
bleeding - damage over time, 1 is how much per interval
poison - damage over time, 1 is how much per interval
shieldmelt - damage over time to shield, 1 is how much per tick
confusion - give confusion status, 1 does nothing
blind - give blind status, 1 does nothing
charm - give charm status, 1 does nothing
fear - give fear status, 1 does nothing
berserk - give berserk status, 1 does nothing
root - give root status, 1 does nothing
petrification - petrify status, 1 does nothing
frozen - frozen status, 1 does nothing
stun - stun status, 1 does nothing
summon - adds the summon status to a summoned creature (which is the status that allows it to remain in the world), 1 does nothing
supercharge - Ursula and Valens' power up skill. 1 does nothing
immortal - grants immortal status, 1 does nothing
doom count - sets doom status, 1 is how many counts of doom (i believe auto-goes down on target's turn)
ambush - basically just cover from archers, i don't think 1 does anything (maybe lets you fire multiple times per ambush?)
ambush interrupt - the "back off" skills, stops the enemy movement as well as ambushes. i don't think 1 does anything (maybe lets you fire multiple times per ambush?)
conditional activate - used in skills like berserker adrenaline that has a condition (like half health) to activate. Requires a subskill to know what to do when the condition is met. The 1 does nothing
always face - always face target, 1 does nothing. Only seems to work on innates
mult shield effectiveness - Multiply shield health, 1 is how much
dropped item chance up - multiply dropped item chance (chance per item to drop when chests can drop), 1 is how much
combatattack damagemult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator does. 1 is the %
combatdefend damagemult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator takes. 1 is the %
combatattack critmult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator does in a crit. 1 is the %
combatdefend critmult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator takes in a crit. 1 is the %
combatdefend defenseadd - flat add or remove a gladiator's defense, 1 is the number
combatattack accuracyadd - flat add or remove a gladiator's accuracy, 1 is the number
combatdefend face front mult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator takes from the front. 1 is the %
combatattack face front mult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator does from the front. 1 is the %
combatdefend face back mult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator takes from the back. 1 is the %
combatattack face back mult - increase/decrease by % the damage a gladiator does from the back. 1 is the %
crowd no flubs - makes it so if you flub a meter, the crowd doesn't care, 1 does nothing
crowd positive mult - multiplies the change to crowd meters you do, 1 is percent
auto regen - set HP regen, number is amount of heal per tick
simple counterattack - grants a .4 damage counter (used on the counter accessories, doesn't require any subskill). 1 is how many counters you do.
immunity status - sets an immunity to a status (which is determined in skillstatuscondition)
immunity counters - immune to counter attacks, 1 does nothing
skill meta cost add - removes or adds SP cost to skills (only seems to work in innates). 1 is by how much in, 10 per SP.
skill meta range add - removes or adds range for targeting, only seems to work in innates. 1 is by how much.
skill meta effect range add - removes or adds area for AoE attacks, only seems to work in innates. 1 is by how much.
skill meta effect mult - not entirely sure, but i think it adds or removes based on the effect (so like, you could make knockback do 2 squares instead of 1), only likely to work in innates. 1 is by how much.
skill meta stack cost mult - removes or adds stack cost to skills (like pass being a half cost turn, you could make it a quarter turn with this), only seems to work in innates. 1 is by how much.
skill meta status duration mult - removes or adds duration to skills, only seems to work in innates. 1 is by how much.
skill meta status effect mult - removes or adds effects on skills (like making poison do more), only seems to work in innates. 1 is by how much. Not sure on this one honestly

All of the below are pretty much the same, either change by a flat number or a %, the 1 is either the flat number or percent to change by.
change moverate
change moverate percent
change power
change power percent
change accuracy
change accuracy percent
change defense
change defense percent
change initiative
change initiative percent
change movetoattack
change movetoattack percent

Durations

SKILLSTATUSDURATION: "Time", 1
SKILLSTATUSINTERVAL: "Time", 1

Both of these use the options below for "Time", then 1 is obviously either how long the status will last or how often the status will tick for things like poison or bleed (interval). Most is self explanatory (stack SUCKS to work with, i advise against it). Source is you, target is your target.

Stack Absolute
Stack Source Mult
Stack Target Mult
Turns Source
Turns Target
Lapse on Failed Condition - used for form buffs, like if you wanted to give a status that only activated when in bear form or something
Lapse with order
Permanent
Until Negated
